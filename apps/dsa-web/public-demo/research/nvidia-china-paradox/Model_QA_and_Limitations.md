Information cutoff: 2026-11-07 23:59 Asia/Shanghai. Valuation comparison date: 2026-11-06 US regular session. The historical research version excludes later financial or policy developments.

## Research judgment

At the archived Nasdaq historical `Close/Last` of USD 186.64, the working Central DCF is USD 157.63/share, about 15.5% lower. The model's Conservative and Long-duration current values are USD 103.42 and USD 215.87. With zero Conservative weight, the quote requires about 49.8% Long-duration and 50.2% Central. With 10%/20% Conservative weight, Long-duration must reach about 59.1%/68.4%. These weights are algebraic illustrations, not empirical probabilities. The author's stance is cautious on valuation pending stronger evidence of post-FY2028 customer returns and cash collection.

The 3×3 matrix holds FY2028 gross margin at 72%, WACC at 10.2025%, the valuation date, opening net assets and estimated diluted shares fixed. Its 70%-growth/Moderated-continuation cell is USD 172.39/share, while its 40%-growth/Long-duration cell is USD 195.15. This supports the duration thesis within the stated model; it does not prove future customer orders.

China access is a strategic option with two regulatory gates. The illustrative USD 1.85/share per USD 10bn durable net-new annual revenue assumes 50% **post-tariff** EBIT margin, 18% tax, 25% terminal ROIC, 2% terminal growth, 10.2% WACC and estimated shares. Linear extrapolation to the USD 41.23/share gap from the separate FY2028-outlook test requires about USD 223bn recurring net-new China sales, beyond FY2026 company revenue. At that scale, linear economics are unlikely to hold; the calculation is a materiality check, not a forecast.

PORTS per-GW content is a **per-generation IT-load benchmark**. Dividing a full fiscal year's company revenue by that benchmark creates a notional content-equivalent ratio only. It cannot establish annual new-campus deployments; company revenue includes other businesses, product content changes, and refresh and installation timing differ. The IEA electricity forecast cannot be compared directly with IT nameplate GW.

## Evidence and calculation checks

- NVIDIA FY2024–FY2026 filed actuals and FY2027 H1 are sourced to SEC 10-K/10-Q; the historical model audit reconciled 175/175 selected values.
- FY2027 Q3 USD 108bn ±2% is management guidance. Q4 USD 105bn and every later operating path are analyst estimates.

NVIDIA H1 non-GAAP net income USD 99.502bn includes SBC under its FY2027 policy; CFO USD 74.421bn is 74.8% of that measure. Issuer-defined H1 free cash flow USD 69.895bn differs from the simple CFO less purchases measure USD 69.987bn by USD 0.092bn of principal payments.
- The forecast's FY2027 unlevered FCFF of USD 104.116bn represents **H2 only**. The DCF begins after the 2026-11-06 close and deducts the estimated pre-close stub.
- OpenAI Group PBC's affiliate is the tenant of the PORTS leases. NVIDIA's USD 105bn cap relates to residual-value guarantees. The disclosed USD 108.5bn maximum gross exposure includes other guarantees. A claim is conditioned on a trigger event and recovery shortfall; neither figure is an expected loss.
- Disclosed supply/capacity commitments of USD 279bn, receivables growth of USD 24.593bn and guarantee caps are different economic and accounting exposures. They must not be added as one debt amount or shown as a causal arrow.
- The 3×3 matrix and isolated Central trial were recalculated in LibreOffice without formula errors; balance-sheet, share, CFO/FCFF, as-of and terminal checks passed in the isolated tests. Native Microsoft Excel full recalculation and a complete capital-allocation review remain required for a signed target/rating.
- An isolated combined stress starts from the matrix's USD 120.80/share FY2028 +40%/Conservative continuation cell. Adding 15 receivable days in FY2028–FY2033 reduces value by about USD 1.05/share; a separate hypothetical USD 10bn net guarantee payment dated 2030-01-27 reduces it by about USD 0.30/share, giving about USD 119.45/share. The payment amount and probability are analyst assumptions, not disclosed liabilities. Receivables affect FCFF through working capital and are not deducted again from equity value.
- The report and Pitch were rendered and visually reviewed. Source references in the report resolve to a source register with primary URLs and publication dates. No post-cutoff external source is used as a historical fact.

## Open limitations at this review stage

1. The market field is an archived Nasdaq `Close/Last`; NOCP and adjustment conventions have not been independently certified.
2. July-to-November cash, securities, debt and diluted shares are analyst rolls, not a 2026-11-06 filing.
3. Central forecast customer orders, hardware ASPs, capacity, margins after FY2028, payout and terminal ROIC require judgment. The forecast accumulates more than USD 2tn cash by FY2033 under the current payout assumptions; this is not added twice to DCF, but the balance-sheet policy is economically weak.
4. No licensed, point-in-time sell-side consensus or matched forward-P/E peer set was archived by the cutoff; the report gives no formal relative-valuation corroboration.
5. A formal BUY/HOLD/SELL rating and 12-month target are withheld until native Excel checks, date-specific price convention and capital-allocation assumptions are signed off. The stated stance is the author's conditional research judgment.
